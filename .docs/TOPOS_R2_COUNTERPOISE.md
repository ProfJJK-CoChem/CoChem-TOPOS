# R2 ordinary DLPNO counterpoise energy contribution

The explicit energy resolution `orca-r2-dlpno-orbital-f12-cp-v1` implements the detailed R2 energy step as **ordinary frozen-core ORCA DLPNO-CCSD(T1)/cc-pVDZ-F12, TightPNO**, with an explicitly selected supported TCutPNO and correlation-fitting basis. The token `cc-pVDZ-F12` names an orbital basis. It does not select an F12 Hamiltonian; this contribution has no CABS or F12 correction. It does not claim MPQC canonical CCSD(T)-F12 equivalence, LED binding energy, or any transferred accuracy benchmark.

[`CorrelatedCounterpoiseProtocol`](../topos/correlated_counterpoise.py) requires the complete native correlated specification and the explicit energy resolution. `run_r2_counterpoise(workflow, record, store, molecule, protocol, deadline, cancel_event)` receives the actual geometry selected by the parent workflow. It never changes the request molecule to hide an intervening geometry calculation. The native-energy helper supplies **one contribution**, not a claim that R2's frozen-monomer optimization and anharmonic requirements are complete.

Exactly two explicitly partitioned, state-resolved closed-shell fragments are supported. The three calculations are:

1. The complex with both physical fragments and the full complex basis.
2. Physical fragment A with its own charge and spin, plus fragment B's ghost basis centers.
3. Physical fragment B with its own charge and spin, plus fragment A's ghost basis centers.

At the unchanged complex geometry, `Delta_E_CP = E_AB^AB - E_A^AB - E_B^AB`. Negative values indicate attraction under this explicit electronic approximation. The two monomers' own-basis energies are not calculated by this three-leg helper, so it reports neither an uncorrected interaction energy nor a BSSE correction magnitude or half-CP estimate. Isolated relaxed monomer references are also absent: this is not a relaxed binding energy, deformation energy, or Gibbs energy.

The physical molecule is kept separate from the full basis-center inventory. Atom IDs, element labels, isotopes, coordinates, fragment membership and charge/spin must match the declared complex partition. The output molecule for each leg is its physical molecule. Ghost centers do not acquire nuclear charge or electrons. Open-shell references, implicit bond cleavage, unsupported ECP scope and arbitrary many-fragment generalizations are rejected. Current scope is all-electron H–Kr in gas phase.

The [ORCA 6.1 counterpoise documentation](https://www.faccts.de/docs/orca/6.1/manual/contents/essentialelements/counterpoise.html) specifies ghost centers with the element-colon syntax. The [basis manual, reading basis sets from a file](https://www.faccts.de/docs/orca/6.1/manual/contents/essentialelements/basisset.html#reading-basis-sets-from-a-file) explicitly documents `GTOName`, `GTOAuxCName` and `GTOAuxJKName` for orbital, correlation-fitting and Coulomb/exchange-fitting bases in GAMESS-US format. The helper uses those fixed input fields and native BASE-authorized `orca_exportbasis` output. All legs receive identical orbital and fitting bytes at every basis center. Native basis receipts bind the named sets, element inventory, exporter, parent ORCA binary, distribution manifest and raw export streams. No user-defined basis file is promoted to a verified named basis by hashing it alone.

Every energy leg is a durable matrix attempt with its own fresh wavefunction/scratch directory, input, full stdout/stderr, artifact hashes, exact protocol and physical molecule. There is no wavefunction transfer between the complex and fragments. Resume verifies retained native evidence, exact deck, shared basis bytes, current executable identity and correlated final energy before reuse. One per-geometry deadline bounds exports and all three legs together, subject to the enclosing deadline and cancellation. A failed or interrupted leg cannot produce a partial interaction sum.

Local tests exercise physical charge/basis-center separation, ordinary-DLPNO method identity, explicit receipt validation and tampering, export authority gates, and cancellation/unavailable-engine behavior. They never simulate a completed licensed calculation. **A genuine three-leg ORCA 6.1.1 calculation and native correlated basis exports remain required for live acceptance.** The parent R2 vibrational treatment is a separate scientific choice; see [the R2 source analysis](TOPOS_R2_MPQC_RESOLUTIONS.md).
